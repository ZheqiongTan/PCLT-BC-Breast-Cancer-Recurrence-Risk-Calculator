About the model & intended use

This tool uses a random survival forest model comprising 200 trees to estimate breast cancer recurrence probabilities at 36 and 60 months. The model was developed using a research cohort of Chinese women with breast cancer. Inputs are limited to TNM stages 0–III. Pathological characteristics and laboratory parameters were assessed before the initiation of anticancer treatment. Predictions are intended for research use and should be interpreted in the context of the study methods, limitations, and target population.

The observed laboratory ranges in the development training set are listed below. These describe data coverage, not laboratory reference intervals. Values outside these ranges trigger a notice and are never silently clipped.
